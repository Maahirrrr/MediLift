Green Corridor Protocol

From airport to OR in 5 steps.

A fully autonomous chain of custody. Zero driver required. Full telemetry at every point.

01
Organ received at airport
Ground crew places bio-pod into MediLift drone bay. RFID-verified handoff. Dispatch confirmed via hospital API.
02
Autonomous flight initiated
Drone lifts off on a pre-approved DGCA airspace corridor at 120m altitude. AI navigates using GPS + barometric sensors.
03
ArUco marker detection
Onboard camera identifies the landing marker on the hospital rooftop. Precision descent begins at 5m horizontal accuracy.
04
Precision landing <0.5m
PD controller steers final descent. Touchdown confirmed. Payload release mechanism disengages the bio-pod automatically.
05
Hospital team collects
Alert sent. Surgical team collects organ pod directly from rooftop pad. Chain-of-custody signed digitally. Drone returns to base.

Safety Framework

If it can fail, we've
planned for it.

Medical logistics demands higher reliability than commercial delivery. Our safety plan is built to aviation-grade standards.

🚧

Geofence & No-Fly Enforcement

Hard-coded geofence boundaries. Drone cannot physically exit permitted corridor. Automatic RTB triggered within 200ms of boundary breach.

⬇️

Low-Altitude Protocol

Cruises at 120m AGL minimum. All maneuvers within controlled sub-150m DGCA exemption zone. No overflights of hospitals or schools.

👨‍✈️

Manual Override Always Active

Remote pilot in command (RPIC) monitors every flight. Full manual override via 4G link. Physical RC backup on missions within 2km range.

❄️

Bio-Payload Integrity

Temperature sensor logs every 10 seconds. Alert at ±1°C deviation. Auto-abort and emergency landing at nearest safe zone if cooling failure detected.
