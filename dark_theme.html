The Mission

Every hour without the organ
costs a life.

A donated heart has a 4-6 hour viability window. Today, road Green Corridors clear traffic but cannot eliminate distance and delays.

💓

Surat → Ahmedabad by air

A donor heart flies from Surat in 45 minutes. But the final 5 km from airport to Civil Hospital still takes 35-50 minutes by road — even on a cleared corridor. MediLift makes it 18 minutes, airborne.

🏥

Surgical team ready on landing

Our temperature-controlled bio-pod maintains 4°C ±0.5°C throughout. The organ arrives at the hospital rooftop pad. The team is already scrubbed and waiting. Zero additional delay.

🛰️

Zero traffic disruption

No road closure. No police escort. No siren. The drone flies at 120m altitude above the city, navigating autonomously via GPS + computer vision. City traffic flows normally below.

📊

Full audit trail

Every mission logs GPS track, altitude, temperature readings, landing accuracy, and timestamps. Full DGCA compliance documentation generated automatically after each flight.

Built for reliability,
not demos.

Precision Landing
ArUco + PD Controller
Rooftop ArUco markers detected at 20m. Proportional-derivative controller achieves sub-50cm landing accuracy. Tested in wind conditions up to 20 km/h.

Peltier TEC unit with PID temperature control. Logs every 10 seconds. Auto-abort and emergency landing if ±1°C deviation detected. 2kg capacity.

Safety Framework

If it can fail,
we've planned for it.

🚧

Geofence Enforcement

Hard-coded 5 km corridor boundaries. Drone cannot exit permitted airspace. RTB triggered within 200ms of breach detection.

⬇️

Low-Altitude Protocol

120m AGL minimum cruise. All maneuvers within sub-150m DGCA exemption zone. No overflights of hospitals, schools, or dense areas.

👨‍✈️

Remote Pilot In Command

Licensed RPIC monitors every flight via 4G GCS link. Full manual override available. Physical RC backup for missions within 2 km range.

❄️

Bio-Payload Integrity

Temperature logged every 10s. Alert at ±1°C. Auto-abort and emergency landing if cooling failure detected. Organ integrity preserved in all scenarios.

⚡

Power Redundancy

X8 octocopter can maintain controlled flight after losing any 2 motors. Dual battery packs. Low-battery RTB trigger at 25% capacity.

📋

DGCA Compliance

NPNT compliant. DigitalSky registered. UAOP filing in progress. Full flight logs submitted to DGCA within 24 hours of each mission.
